DhandhoPOS Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

In short: your shop's data stays on your phone. DhandhoPOS has no account and no cloud server that receives your sales. After its first 14 days the app shows ads from Google, and Google collects some information about your phone, not about your shop, to choose and count them. The app also sends a product's barcode number when you scan one to add a new item.

Who we are

DhandhoPOS is a billing and stock app for shops, published on Google Play by Buildanta. In this policy "we" means Buildanta, and "the app" means DhandhoPOS.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your own phone, which other apps cannot read. This includes:

  • your shop's details: name, address, phone number, GSTIN and UPI ID;
  • products, prices, stock, and any product photos you take;
  • sales, payments, refunds, shifts and reports;
  • customers you choose to add, such as their name, phone number, and loyalty or credit balance;
  • staff names, employee IDs and roles. PINs and PIN-recovery answers are stored only as salted hashes, never as the numbers or words themselves.

We do not receive any of this. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

What leaves your phone

  • Ads. For its first 14 days the app shows no ads. After that it shows ads through Google AdMob: one when you open the app, one after you close a shift, and up to two more a day that play only if you choose to watch them, which opens the stock tools for that day. No ad shows while you are billing a sale. To show and count ads, Google's ads software collects your phone's advertising ID and other device identifiers, its IP address (which shows roughly where the phone is), details of the phone and of this app, how the app performs, and how you use the ads themselves, such as whether one was opened or watched. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and preventing fraud, and may use the advertising ID to show ads based on your interests. Nothing about your shop goes to Google: no sales, items, customers or staff. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, and turn off ads based on your interests, in your phone's Settings under Google, then Ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.
  • Looking up a new product. When you scan a barcode to add a product, the app sends only that barcode number to the public product databases Open Food Facts (with Open Products Facts and Open Beauty Facts) and, if they do not know it, UPCitemdb, to suggest a name. No shop, sales, customer or staff data is sent. Like any internet request, these services see your phone's IP address. This never happens while you are billing a sale.
  • The barcode scanner. Scanning uses Google's ML Kit, which reads the barcode on your phone. ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information, such as the phone model, Android version, app version, performance figures, error codes and a per-installation identifier. It does not send camera images or what a barcode says. Google's privacy policy applies to that information.
  • Things you choose to send. When you share a receipt on WhatsApp, share a receipt or backup file, or message or call support, the app hands it to the app you pick, and it goes to whoever you send it to. We do not receive it.

Permissions

  • Camera: scanning barcodes and photographing products.
  • Bluetooth and Nearby devices: finding and printing on a receipt printer. The app never uses this to work out where you are.
  • USB: printing on a receipt printer plugged into the phone.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock: optional, to reset a forgotten owner PIN. Android checks it; the app never receives your fingerprint.
  • Internet: the product lookup and the ads described above.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ads software, as described above.

Backups

A backup is a single encrypted file that you save or share yourself. We never receive it. Anyone who has your backup file can restore it into DhandhoPOS, so keep it as private as your account books.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing for us to delete. A phone lock keeps it safe if the phone is lost.

Children

DhandhoPOS is a tool for running a business. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version works differently, for example by adding online backup, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above. Ads were added on 7 October 2026.
